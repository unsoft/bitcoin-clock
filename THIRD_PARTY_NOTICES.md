## LCD Mono fonts in SLS assets

The font folders under `SLS/assets/Fonts/lcd_lcd_mono/` contain their own
`LICENSE.TXT` and `README.TXT` files. Their license requires preservation of
the original notices and a source URL when redistributing the fonts as part of
a larger software distribution. The license identifies the source URL as
<http://www.spinwardstars.com/scrfonts/>. Review these terms before including
or redistributing those design assets.

## Other SLS fonts

`SLS/assets/Fonts/modern_lcd_7/` identifies Modern LCD-7 by Sizenko Alexander
(Style-7) as freeware for home use, with credit required when used in freeware
software; commercial use requires a separate license. See its
[`readme.txt`](SLS/assets/Fonts/modern_lcd_7/readme.txt). Other fonts bundled
under `SLS/assets/Fonts/` have not all been individually reviewed here. Check
their included license/readme and obtain any required permissions before
redistributing those assets, especially for commercial use.
